# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Argue about code, not about each other. Assume the person on the
other end is trying to help.

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project — filing an issue, opening a pull request,
answering a question, or reviewing someone else's work — is entitled to do so without
being harassed or belittled, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What helps

- Reviewing the change rather than the person who wrote it.
- Saying what you think is wrong and **why**, with enough detail to act on.
- Accepting that a maintainer may decline a change, and that a contributor may decline
  to make one.
- Taking a "that is wrong, here is the measurement" as a gift. This project's history
  is full of confidently-wrong claims that were corrected by someone bothering to
  measure; that correction is the most valuable thing a contributor does.
- Asking a beginner's question without apologising for it.

## What does not

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
- Insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Harassment, public or private.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Treating a mistake as evidence of someone's worth as an engineer.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, code comments — and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer by
[opening a private security advisory](https://github.com/wynlang/wyn/security/advisories/new)
(the only private channel this repository has; it is not only for vulnerabilities) or
by contacting the maintainer directly through their GitHub profile.

Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer will respond with what action, if
any, is being taken. Responses available include a private warning, editing or removing
the offending content, and blocking an account from the repository. The maintainer is
also subject to this document; a complaint about the maintainer's own conduct is not an
invalid complaint.

Because this is a single-maintainer project there is no committee and no appeal body.
That is a real limitation and it is stated rather than hidden.
